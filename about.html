Odisha Adarsha Vidyalaya

Odisha Adarsha Vidyalaya (OAV) is an ambitious Government of Odisha initiative created to provide quality education to talented students, especially from rural and semi-urban areas.

OAV provides an English-medium learning environment based on the CBSE curriculum. The programme began in the academic year 2016–17 with 100 schools in 100 blocks.

OAV focuses on the overall development of students through academics, laboratories, libraries, information and communication technology, sports, arts, music, computer education and other co-curricular activities.

Odisha Adarsha Vidyalayas are CBSE-affiliated schools. The regular entry level is Class VI, while admission to higher classes may take place through the prescribed lateral-entry process when seats are available.
